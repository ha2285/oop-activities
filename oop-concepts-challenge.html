Pet Park: design the classes

You helped Dad build Shahd’s Bubble Pop. Now Shahd has a new game, and this time you are the designer. Draw the class diagram on the canvas, then submit to see how your design meets her requirements.

S
Shahd → you
“My next game is Pet Park! I want cats, fish, parrots and ducks. They all have names and get hungry, and each one makes its own sound when I tap it. Fish swim, parrots fly, and ducks do both. And please add a Pool party button for everyone who can swim!”
Requirements
  1. Every pet has a name and a hunger level, written once (not copied into each pet).
  2. Hunger must always stay between 0 and 100: no outside code may set it directly.
  3. Every pet makes its own sound, and nobody may create a plain “pet” with no sound.
  4. Fish and ducks can swim; parrots and ducks can fly. Cats do neither.
  5. The Pool party button must be able to treat every swimmer the same way, whatever kind of pet it is.
The canvas starts with Cat, Fish, Parrot and Duck. You have 5 minutes.
